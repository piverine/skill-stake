# Skill-Stake Demo Video Script

**Tone**: Energetic, professional, and slightly provocative (referencing the "stake" aspect).
**Estimated Duration**: 60-90 seconds.

---

### **Section 1: The Problem & The Hook (0:00 - 0:15)**

**[Visual Cue]**: 
*   Montage of a user looking bored at a screen, switching tabs to YouTube/social media, a pile of unread PDF documents.
*   *Text Overlay*: "Procrastination is expensive."

**[Voiceover]**: 
"We all have that folder of courses we swore we’d finish. But let's be honest—without skin in the game, it’s just digital clutter. Motivation fades. Procrastination wins."

---

### **Section 2: The Solution (0:15 - 0:25)**

**[Visual Cue]**: 
*   Screen recording: Landing page of **Skill-Stake**. Fast cuts of the "Stake" button and "AI Quiz" generation.
*   *Text Overlay*: "Skill-Stake: Learn or Pay."

**[Voiceover]**: 
"Enter **Skill-Stake**. The platform that turns learning into a commitment. We combine AI-powered testing with blockchain accountability to ensure you actually *learn* what you read."

---

### **Section 3: The Workflow (0:25 - 0:55)**

**[Visual Cue]**: 
*   User uploading a PDF file (e.g., "Introduction to Python").
*   A "Processing..." animation/loader.

**[Voiceover]**: 
"Here’s how it works. First, upload your study material. Our AI integration, powered by **Gemini**, instantly analyzes your document to understand the core concepts."

**[Visual Cue]**: 
*   User clicking a "Stake ETH" button. MetaMask pop-up confirming a transaction.
*   Dashboard showing "Locked Stake: 0.01 ETH".

**[Voiceover]**: 
"Now, prove you’re serious. Stake a small amount of ETH into the smart contract. This isn't a fee—it's a pledge. Your money is now locked until you prove you’ve learned the material."

**[Visual Cue]**: 
*   User taking a quiz. Selecting answers. 
*   Quick cuts of different questions.

**[Voiceover]**: 
"The system generates a custom quiz based on your upload. No generic questions—this checks if you *really* read your specific document."

---

### **Section 4: The Stakes & Conclusion (0:55 - 1:15)**

**[Visual Cue]**: 
*   **Scene A (Success)**: Screen shows "PASSED: 90%". Animation of ETH returning to wallet. Green checkmarks.
*   **Scene B (Failure - optional to show)**: "FAILED". Red X. "Stake Lost".

**[Voiceover]**: 
"Pass the quiz? Smart contract triggers, and your stake is refunded instantly to your wallet. You keep your money and gain the knowledge."

**[Visual Cue]**: 
*   Logo animation: **Skill-Stake**.
*   *Text Overlay*: "Tech Stack: Next.js • FastAPI • Gemini • Ethereum".

**[Voiceover]**: 
"But fail? Well... let's just say that’s the price of laziness. Stop hoarding tutorials. Start staking your skills. This is **Skill-Stake**."
